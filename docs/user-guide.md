# NEXORA Enterprise User Guide

Welcome to NEXORA, your comprehensive Warehouse Management & Inventory ERP platform.

## Navigation Guide

### 🏠 Dashboard
Displays real-time operational overview:
- Total Stock Count, Total Stock Value, Pending Inbound Receipts, Low Stock Alerts.
- Stock Movement Charts and Quick Action widgets.

### 📦 Master Data
Manage core system entities before processing transactions:
- **Products**: Manage item catalog, SKUs, barcode numbers, prices, and stock thresholds.
- **Categories & Units**: Group products and define measurement units.
- **Suppliers & Customers**: Partner database and contact information.
- **Warehouses & Locations**: Set up storage sites and bin locations.

### 🔄 Inventory Management
- **Stock**: Real-time stock levels per warehouse location.
- **Stock Movement**: Track every item movement (Inbound, Outbound, Transfer).
- **Stock Opname**: Perform stock audit counts and record variance adjustments.
- **Slow Moving**: Identify non-moving items to prevent dead stock accumulation.

### 🛒 Procurement & Outbound Operations
- **Procurement**: Workflow for Purchase Requests, PO generation, and Goods Receipt receiving.
- **Outbound**: Material Requests, Order Picking, Packing verification, Delivery Orders, and Shipment tracking.

### 📊 Analytics & AI
- **Demand Forecast**: AI-driven sales and stock demand predictions.
- **Reorder Recommendations**: Automated suggestions on when and how much stock to order.
- **Inventory Health Score**: Key metrics on turnover rate, stockout risks, and capacity utilization.
